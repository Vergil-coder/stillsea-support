Stillsea support

Need help with your timer, fish or a purchase? Email the developer at vergilluw@gmail.com.

If something went wrong, include your iPhone model, iOS version, Stillsea version (at the bottom of Settings), what you tapped and what happened. A screenshot is optional. Please leave out payment details and private journal entries.

Common questions

How do I get my first focus egg?

Complete at least 10 minutes of eligible focus in one session. A completed 25-minute session earns a Wave Egg. Open the egg at the end of the session to meet your fish.

Where is my timer on the Lock Screen?

Open Notifications & Lock Screen in Stillsea Settings. Check the Live Activities and notification permissions in iPhone Settings. Start a countdown and lock the screen. Notification and Focus settings can affect which alerts you see.

How do I move my fish to a new iPhone?

Use the same Apple Account on both phones and enable iCloud Sync in Stillsea. Keep your old app installed until you can see your sessions and fish on the new phone. Growth photos stay on the original device.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the membership screen in Settings and tap Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used to pay. Active subscriptions and eligible one-time unlocks can be restored. Scale packs are consumables. If a completed order is missing, reopen Stillsea and contact support before buying again.

How do I manage my subscription?

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and select Stillsea. Apple shows your renewal date and cancellation options.

How do I save a fish card?

Open the fish in your collection and choose a sharing or saving option. When saving to Photos, allow Stillsea to add images. You can review this permission in iPhone Settings.

常见问题

专注多久能获得鱼卵？

单次有效专注满 10 分钟开始有卵。完整专注 25 分钟可获得海浪卵，结束后点开即可孵化。

锁屏看不到计时怎么办？

在 App「设置 → 通知与锁屏」查看状态，再在 iPhone 设置中检查实时活动和通知权限。开始倒计时后锁屏查看；系统专注模式也会影响提醒显示。

换手机后怎么找回鱼群？

两台手机登录同一 Apple 账户，在 App 设置中开启 iCloud 同步。等新机出现原来的鱼群和记录后再处理旧机。成长照片只保存在原设备。

如何恢复购买？

使用购买时的 Apple 账户，在设置里的会员页面点「恢复购买」。有效订阅和符合条件的买断权益可恢复。鳞片包为消耗品，如订单已完成但余额没有增加，请重新打开 App，仍未到账可发邮件查询。

怎样取消续订？

打开 iPhone「设置 → 你的姓名 → 订阅」，选择专注鱼鱼，可查看续订日期及取消选项。

鱼卡怎么保存到相册？

在图鉴里打开鱼的详情，选择分享或保存。首次存入相册时允许 App 添加照片，之后也可在 iPhone 设置中调整。
