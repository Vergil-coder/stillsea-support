Privacy policy

Mellowfin for iPhone · Updated November 25, 2026

You can focus, hatch fish and keep a journal without creating a Mellowfin account. The app has no advertising, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking.

Your ocean and journal

Your iPhone stores your sessions, notes, fish names, collection, scale balance and settings so the app can run your timer and keep your progress. Growth photos are stored on the device where you took them.

iCloud and your choices

When iCloud is available, sync is enabled by default. Mellowfin uses Apple’s private iCloud key-value storage to sync supported progress between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The developer has no server copy of your journal or collection.

You can pause sync in Mellowfin Settings under iCloud Sync. Pausing stops further syncing and leaves existing copies in place. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Manage data held by iCloud in your Apple Account settings. Items you saved to Photos can be deleted in Photos.

Purchases

Apple handles payment. Mellowfin uses StoreKit product and verified transaction information to unlock purchases, credit scales once and restore eligible entitlements. Your payment card details are handled by Apple.

Notifications, photos and sharing

With your permission, Mellowfin schedules local timer reminders. Live Activities can show your timer on the Lock Screen. You control these in iPhone Settings. Saving a card, Live Photo or ocean clip requests permission to add to Photos. When you share, you choose the destination in the system share sheet.

Support and this website

If you email vergilluw@gmail.com, the developer receives the address, message and attachments you send. These are used to answer and follow up on your request. You can use the same address to ask for deletion of this correspondence. Include only information you are comfortable sharing.

These pages are designed for GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as visitors’ IP addresses to operate and secure the site. These pages use no analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub’s privacy statement · Apple’s privacy policy

专注、孵鱼和记录手帐无需注册。App 没有广告、第三方统计或跨 App 追踪。

专注记录与收藏

专注记录、手帐、鱼的昵称、收藏、鳞片余额和设置保存在设备上，供计时和记录进度使用。成长照片保存在拍摄它的设备上。

iCloud 与数据管理

iCloud 可用时默认开启同步。App 使用 Apple 的私有键值存储，在同一 Apple 账户的设备间同步支持的进度。开发者的服务器不保存你的手帐或收藏。

可在 App「设置 → iCloud 同步」中暂停。暂停会停止后续同步，已有副本会保留。删除 App 会移除本地应用数据。iCloud 中的数据可在 Apple 账户设置里管理。已保存到「照片」的作品可在相册中删除。

购买

Apple 负责付款。App 使用 StoreKit 的商品和已验证交易信息来解锁权益、为每笔订单发放一次鳞片，以及恢复符合条件的购买。银行卡信息由 Apple 处理。

通知、相册与分享

经你允许，App 会在本机安排计时提醒。实时活动可在锁屏显示计时，可在 iPhone 设置中管理。保存卡片、实况照片或短片时会申请向相册添加作品的权限。分享时，接收方由你在系统分享面板中选择。
